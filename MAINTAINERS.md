# Maintainers

OWP is in seed stage. Maintainer authority is held by individuals, not transferable corporate seats.

## Seed rule

Before the project has three unaffiliated active maintainers, seed maintainers may merge changes required to establish the protocol and reference implementation. All normative decisions must remain public in Git.

## Graduation rule

Once three unaffiliated maintainers are active, no single employer may hold a majority of active maintainer votes. New maintainers are selected for sustained technical contribution, cross-organization judgment, and demonstrated commitment to vendor-neutral interoperability.

Employer affiliation is disclosed for transparency but does not create a corporate veto or permanent seat.
