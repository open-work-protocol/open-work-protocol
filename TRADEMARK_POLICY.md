# OWP Name and Trademark Policy — Seed Stage

"Open Work Protocol" and "OWP" are used here as project/specification identifiers. No compatibility claim implies endorsement by Keeltrace, any future foundation, A2A, AAIF, or another standards project.

Until a neutral steward adopts a formal trademark policy:

- anyone may accurately describe an implementation as implementing or being compatible with a published OWP version;
- no one may imply certification, endorsement, or official status that has not been granted;
- the protocol name may be changed before 1.0 if clearance or neutral-governance needs require it;
- founders must not use mark control to prevent lawful compatible implementations or forks.

A future neutral steward should own or administer project marks separately from technical conformance and provide fair, published usage rules.
