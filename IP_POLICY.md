# OWP Intellectual Property Policy

Status: pre-foundation community policy for the experimental specification and reference implementation.

## Outbound license

OWP specification text, schemas, reference code, tests, examples, and conformance material are made available under Apache License 2.0 unless a file explicitly states otherwise.

Apache-2.0 includes a contributor patent license for patent claims necessarily infringed by the contributor's contribution, subject to the license terms. OWP does not add a separate patent pool, royalty schedule, or field-of-use restriction.

## Inbound contributions

Contributions are accepted under the same Apache-2.0 terms and should carry Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off. The project does not require copyright assignment to a founder or vendor.

Contributors must have the right to submit their contribution. A contributor aware that a proposed normative feature necessarily depends on third-party patent, trademark, or other rights that would prevent royalty-free implementation should disclose that issue during review.

## Specification implementation rights

Any person or organization may implement the published specification, commercially or non-commercially, without obtaining permission from a founder, maintainer, broker, or member organization, subject to applicable licenses and law.

## Neutral-home migration

If OWP enters a neutral foundation or standards organization, this policy may be replaced by that organization's approved IP policy. Migration must not retroactively create a proprietary implementation toll or transfer contributors' copyrights to a commercial founder.
