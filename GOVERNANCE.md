# Governance

OWP begins as a seed proposal and aims to become public, multi-vendor infrastructure.

## Stage 0 — Seed

Founding maintainers may move quickly to prove real interoperability. Protocol-semantic changes should be public and covered by tests/fixtures.

No copyright-assignment CLA is required. Contributions should use Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (`git commit -s`).

## Stage 1 — Multi-maintainer

Trigger: at least three independent implementations or three unaffiliated organizations making substantive protocol contributions.

No organization should control more than half of maintainer seats. Breaking wire changes require public RFC review and supermajority maintainer approval.

## Stage 2 — Neutral home

When ecosystem adoption warrants it, maintainers should move protocol/trademark governance to a neutral foundation, standards project, or dedicated nonprofit/project organization if that improves trust.

## Rules

- Unknown optional extension fields remain forward-compatible.
- Breaking schema changes require migration notes and conformance fixtures.
- Security fixes may be temporarily embargoed.
- Trademark control must not block compatible independent implementations.
- No required private infrastructure or secret conformance suite.


## Standards-grade safeguards

- Technical participation is individual and contribution-based; employer affiliation does not buy technical control.
- OWP does not publish market pricing, fee, margin, or customer-allocation guidance. See `ANTITRUST.md`.
- Inbound/outbound IP rules are public. See `IP_POLICY.md`.
- Seed marks must not be used to exclude compatible implementations. See `TRADEMARK_POLICY.md`.
- A future foundation transfer may change administrative governance but must preserve free implementation rights and public technical process.
