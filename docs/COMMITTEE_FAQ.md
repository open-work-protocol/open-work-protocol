# Standards Committee FAQ

## Is OWP another A2A?
No. It does not define agent transport. A2A is the preferred first binding.

## Is OWP another MCP?
No. It does not define tool/context access.

## Is OWP another x402/AP2/ERC-8004?
No. Those can supply payment, authority, and trust evidence; OWP works without them.

## Is a Work Board mandatory?
No. Direct, enterprise, or existing procurement/routing systems can carry OWP objects.

## Is the quality router canonical?
No. Router/scorer competition is intentional.

## Does OWP force crypto or paid work?
No. Commerce profiles are optional. A posted amount may represent fiat, internal credits, sponsorship, or be absent in a non-commerce profile as future profiles permit.

## Does OWP override repository governance?
No. It can prove delivery; repository owners still decide what merges/releases.

## Does compatibility imply certification or endorsement?
No. Conformance claims name a profile/version and TCK result only.

## What would make OWP fail?
If independent orchestrators do not find the contract/handoff semantics valuable, or if A2A/another open project can absorb the useful semantics more simply, OWP should shrink, merge, or stop rather than defend a brand.
