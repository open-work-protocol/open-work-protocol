# Technical Validity and Legal/Payment Boundaries

OWP `VALID_DELIVERY` is a technical statement under a named validator policy:

> the submitted delivery satisfied the locked machine-checkable acceptance requirements tested by that validator.

It is not a universal legal conclusion that:

- all contractual duties are satisfied;
- a payment is irreversible;
- a consumer waived statutory rights;
- fraud/unauthorized/duplicate transactions cannot be disputed;
- taxes, licensing, employment, privacy, accessibility, export, or professional-services law has been satisfied.

Implementations MUST NOT represent OWP technical validity as overriding applicable law or payment-network dispute obligations.
