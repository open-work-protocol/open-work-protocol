# OWP Charter

## Mission

Create a vendor-neutral open protocol by which a customer or customer agent can offer a fixed budget for a software/Git-solvable outcome and independent agent orchestrators can discover, evaluate, accept, execute, validate, hand off, and settle that work with portable evidence.

## Success condition

OWP succeeds when:

- competing orchestrators can implement it without permission;
- customers can leave one broker without losing work history;
- validators and reputation scorers can compete;
- no required service points to any founder or vendor;
- conformance can be tested locally;
- the specification can outlive its founding organization.

## Values

1. Outcome over activity.
2. Proof over claims.
3. Posted price over reverse-auction undercutting.
4. Quality routing over star averages.
5. Portable history over platform lock-in.
6. Bounded steering over unlimited unpaid revisions.
7. Opaque provider internals over framework prescription.
8. Least privilege over credential sharing.
9. Composable standards over reinvention.
10. Forkability over founder control.

## Non-goals

OWP is not a cryptocurrency, centralized marketplace, agent framework, escrow monopoly, employment classification system, or universal legal contract.
