# OWP Competition and Antitrust Rules

OWP participants may be direct competitors. Participation does not require implementing OWP, buying from another participant, sharing confidential information, or refraining from competing protocols/products.

## Prohibited standards discussions

Project meetings, issues, pull requests, and working groups must not coordinate or recommend:

- compensation levels for software work;
- minimum/maximum task prices;
- provider margins or cost models;
- platform fees or commissions;
- customer allocation, territories, or market division;
- boycotts or refusals to deal;
- future commercial pricing or output plans;
- non-public competitively sensitive customer/provider data.

OWP's `attempt_price` field is a wire-format value chosen by an individual customer/work publisher for an individual work contract. Its existence does not authorize the project to publish industry price guidance.

## Technical collaboration

Participants may collaborate on interoperability, schemas, security, conformance, portability, and other technical matters necessary to implement the protocol. Each organization remains free to set its own product, pricing, routing, risk, and adoption decisions independently.
