# RFC-0001 — Core Work Contract

Status: Seed.

A2A can represent a Task, but paid outcome work also needs immutable economics, acceptance, revision rights, validation, and handoff.

Proposal: adopt the seven OWP objects plus hash-linked WorkEvent.

Key choice: customer posts price; public ProviderDecision is ACCEPT/PASS only.

Reverse auction is rejected because it rewards low bids and encourages providers to understate uncertain execution cost.
