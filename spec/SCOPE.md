# OWP Scope Boundary

## Normative problem owned by OWP

OWP standardizes **portable outcome-work semantics**:

- what outcome is offered;
- posted per-attempt economics (if commerce is used);
- provider `ACCEPT` / `PASS` decision;
- locked acceptance/evidence requirements;
- immutable attempt identity and revision lineage;
- provider delivery claim;
- independent validation result;
- customer `APPROVE` / `STEER` / `REJECT` disposition;
- provider `REMEDY` / `PASS` response to a paid revision;
- portable handoff between providers.

## Explicitly not owned by OWP

OWP does **not** standardize:

- agent-to-agent transport, streaming, RPC, task queues, or discovery;
- model/tool/context integration;
- identity credentials or authentication protocols;
- payment networks, custody, escrow, or settlement finality;
- user spending authority;
- universal reputation or a canonical quality score;
- Git hosting, source control, CI/CD, or package registries;
- orchestrator internal architecture, prompts, models, workers, or cost accounting;
- market-clearing prices, provider bidding, commissions, or recommended fees;
- legal employment classification, jurisdiction, warranty, or consumer rights.

OWP profiles MAY bind these external capabilities without absorbing their ownership.
